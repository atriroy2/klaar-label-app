Kavita: Dashboard beta launch date. We were aiming for March 15th. It's now the 16th.

Deepa: We shipped yesterday actually. Late evening, but it went out.

Kavita: Okay, a day slip. Why?

Deepa: The chart component. Ananya realized there was a visual inconsistency and she wanted to tweak it. It took longer than expected.

Kavita: This was post-freeze.

Deepa: Yeah. I know you said we needed to escalate post-freeze changes, and I should have. I'm sorry.

Kavita: What was the change?

Deepa: The axis labels were rendering with inconsistent font weights across the three chart types. It was bugging Ananya and frankly, it was bugging me too.

Kavita: How long did the fix take?

Deepa: Ishaan spent a day on it. And then there was QA time. That's where the slip came from.

Kavita: Ishaan spent a full day on font weight inconsistency.

Deepa: When you say it like that, it sounds silly.

Kavita: I'm not judging silly. I'm asking: was this worth a day of Ishaan's time one week before launch?

Deepa: [pause] In hindsight, no. It was polish. The beta didn't need to be perfect.

Kavita: Exactly. It was perfectionism. And it cost a day. We were already tight. What would've happened if you'd left it as is?

Deepa: We would've shipped on time. And honestly, our users probably wouldn't have noticed or cared.

Kavita: Right. So this is the thing we talked about in January — the design spiral. And you made the call to change something without escalating it to me.

Deepa: I know. I should've flagged it.

Kavita: I'm not mad. But I need to understand why you didn't. Was it because you thought I'd say no?

Deepa: Partly. But also, I was already nervous about the timeline and I thought fixing it would just take a couple hours. Then it took longer than I thought.

Kavita: So you made a call without full information and it slipped. That's the pattern. Help me figure out how to prevent it next time.

Deepa: I think I need to actually flag it. Like, even if I think it's small, I should message you and wait for your okay.

Kavita: What would that conversation look like?

Deepa: I'd say, "Hey, Ananya found an issue with the chart axis labels. It'll take Ishaan a day to fix. Should we do it?" And then you'd say yes or no.
