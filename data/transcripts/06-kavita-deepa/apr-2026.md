Kavita: Q2 planning. I want to start with a design philosophy conversation before we talk roadmap.

Deepa: Okay, what's on your mind?

Kavita: After the dashboard project, I realized we need structure around design changes. Not to limit creativity, but to avoid the thing that happened in March.

Deepa: The late polish spiral.

Kavita: Right. So I want to propose something. A design freeze rule that's hard. All design changes after a certain date need written approval. No exceptions.

Deepa: Written approval sounds formal.

Kavita: It is. That's the point. It forces a conversation instead of a decision. And it creates a record. Here's what I'm thinking: one week before launch, no design changes without VP sign-off. Changes have to be documented — what changed, why, what's the impact on timeline.

Deepa: One week?

Kavita: Is that too long to freeze? Can you live with your designs for a week?

Deepa: I mean, I can. It's just going to be hard when I see something that's not quite right.

Kavita: What if we do the pre-freeze review like we talked about? The day before freeze, we sit down for an hour. You and I look at the designs together. You can make one final pass at improvements. Then it locks.

Deepa: How thorough can that pass be?

Kavita: Not a redesign. Tweaks. Color adjustments, spacing, typography choices. Things you can do in a day or two, not a week.

Deepa: And after that hour?

Kavita: Locked. Anything else is a post-freeze change that goes through the approval process.

Deepa: What about actual bugs? Like, if there's a rendering issue that we missed?

Kavita: That's not a design change, that's a fix. That goes through fast. But polish and refinement — that's the freeze.

Deepa: Okay. I can commit to that.

Kavita: Good. What projects do we have in Q2?

Deepa: The GA launch for the dashboard, the bundle size optimization, and we're planning a new feature on the metrics configuration. Oh, and we want to start work on a mobile app prototype.

Kavita: Four things. That's too many. What's the priority?

Deepa: Dashboard GA is number one. Bundle size is number two because it affects the GA experience. Mobile prototype is lower priority.

Kavita: And metrics configuration?

Deepa: That's new work that product wants. But if we're already at capacity, it could slide to Q3.

Kavita: Let's be clear. You've got dashboard GA, bundle size, and mobile prototype exploration. That's three big things. Metrics configuration is Q3.

Deepa: Okay.

Kavita: Dashboard GA. When do you want to ship?

Deepa: May 15th ideally.

Kavita: May 15th. That's six weeks from now. Is that the design freeze date or the ship date?

Deepa: Ship date. So freeze date would be... May 8th?

Kavita: And when do we do the pre-freeze review?

Deepa: May 7th?

Kavita: Perfect. So May 7th we review together, May 8th everything freezes, May 15th we ship. What needs to happen to make that real?

Deepa: Ishaan and the team need to stabilize the performance. We're at 72 Lighthouse, we want to get to 80 for GA.

Kavita: What's the gap?

Deepa: Bundle size is the main thing. There's also some rendering optimization Ishaan wants to do.

Kavita: Is 80 achievable by May 8th?

Deepa: The rendering optimization is done. Bundle size might take a few more weeks.

Kavita: Okay, so we're looking at 75-76 by freeze, then we iterate on bundle size in the final week?

Deepa: Yeah, that's realistic.

Kavita: That works. So the target is 75+ Lighthouse at freeze, GA launch May 15th. Agreed?

Deepa: Agreed.

Kavita: Good. What about the customization feature? Is that shipping with GA or after?

Deepa: That ships with GA. It's already built, we just need to test it thoroughly.

Kavita: Okay, so the GA feature set is locked. Dashboard redesign, customization, Lighthouse 75+. Anything else?

Deepa: Those are the big things.

Kavita: And your team? Same lineup?

Deepa: Ishaan's leading again. Arjun and Pooja. We might pull in one more person for the bundle size optimization work since it's specialized.

Kavita: Who?

Deepa: There's a senior in Vikram's team who knows web performance really well. Priya, I think?

Kavita: Priya's pretty full with backend work, but let me check with Vikram. If he can spare her for part of Q2, she'd be great for that.

Deepa: Okay. If not, we'll figure it out.

Kavita: Alright, mobile prototype. What's the vision?

Deepa: Just exploring whether a mobile app makes sense. Functionality parity with the web dashboard, but optimized for mobile use cases.

Kavita: Timeline?

Deepa: May-June exploration. Nothing shipping by end of Q2.

Kavita: Okay. That's good. That's exploration work. Who's leading that?

Deepa: I'm thinking of having Ananya explore the design side. Give her something different than dashboard work.

Kavita: How much time is she spending on it?

Deepa: That's it.

Kavita: I like this plan. It's realistic and it has structure. The design freeze rule is going to be important. You're going to feel the urge to change things. When you do, send me a message. I'll say yes or no. Fair?
