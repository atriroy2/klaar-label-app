Kavita: Q1 review time. Let's start with the collab engine. Where are we?

Rohan: We're tracking well for July. The sync engine is solid, Priya's storage layer is clean, and Ishaan's actually shipping features independently now.

Kavita: Shipping features? Like, without you writing code?

Rohan: Yeah. He did the reconnection logic last week. I reviewed it, it was solid. Some tweaks but fundamentally good design.

Kavita: That's a massive jump from where he was in January.

Rohan: He's smart. He just needed the right problem and the mentoring. He's going to be a good Staff Engineer.

Kavita: I can see that. So the mentoring OKR is tracking.

Rohan: Yeah. Ishaan's clearly on the path. And I've been doing some architecture guidance with Vikram's team too.

Kavita: What kind of guidance? Are they coming to you regularly?

Rohan: They'll reach out when they hit a design question. I've had a couple of sync calls with Deepa about the migration architecture, and Vikram asked me about caching strategies last week.

Kavita: Good. That's starting to happen naturally. I want to come back to the mentoring piece though. You've mentioned a few times that you're "supporting" people. I want to understand what that looks like.

Rohan: I review their code, we have design discussions, I make sure the architecture is sound.

Kavita: Are they presenting their work?

Rohan: To the team, sometimes. To me, for review.

Kavita: Not to the broader org?

Rohan: Not usually.

Kavita: Why is that?

Rohan: [pause] I guess because I'm more comfortable presenting it. I can explain it better.

Kavita: Rohan, I want to be direct with you. Your mentoring style could be more empowering.

Rohan: What do you mean?

Kavita: I mean that when Ishaan builds something good, Ishaan should be the one talking about it. Not you supporting him. Not you explaining it better.

Rohan: But I can explain it better than he can.

Kavita: Probably. But is that the goal? To be the person who explains things? Or is it to help him be able to explain things?

Rohan: [pause] The second one.

Kavita: Right. So let me give you an example. Priya's webhook retry system a few weeks ago?

Rohan: That was good work.

Kavita: It was. Who presented it at the architecture review?

Rohan: [pause] I did.

Kavita: Was Priya in the room?

Rohan: ...yeah.

Kavita: So Priya designed it, engineered it, and then you presented it.

Rohan: I was making sure the group understood the design choices.

Kavita: I'm sure you explained it well. But Priya's probably thinking, "I built this but my tech lead doesn't think I can talk about it."

Rohan: I wasn't trying to send that message.

Kavita: I know. But that's what gets received. So here's my ask. From now on, when someone on your team builds something, they present it. You're in the room, you're ready to jump in if needed, but they own the presentation.

Rohan: Even if they're nervous?

Kavita: Especially if they're nervous. That's how they get better. And they need the practice.

Rohan: Okay. I can do that. I never thought about it from that angle, honestly.

Kavita: Good. It's a small thing but it changes the mentoring dynamic. You're coaching them to be independent, not just be the smart person in the room.

Rohan: I hear you.

Kavita: How is the team morale overall? You mentioned Ishaan's thriving, what about Priya?

Rohan: Priya's solid. She's taking on more complex work. She's less outgoing than Ishaan but her technical thinking is sharp.

Kavita: Is she getting enough mentoring?

Rohan: I think so. She's been asking better questions lately. But you're right — she should be the one talking about her work, not me.

Kavita: Exactly. Alright. On the comp side — I had the conversation with our CFO. We can do a 22% raise for you. Not quite closing the gap to market, but it's a real move.

Rohan: 22%?

Kavita: Yeah. You're critical to our technical strategy and the mentoring OKR is real. This is what we can do now.

Rohan: That's... better than I expected, honestly.

Kavita: Good. You deserve it. And we're not done. As we grow and as your scope increases, we'll revisit. But this is where we are for now.

Rohan: Okay. Thank you.

Kavita: Start with 2-3 hours a week. Office hours, design reviews, maybe some Slack discussions. It'll probably grow as the company does.

Rohan: I can manage that alongside collab engine work.

Kavita: Good. You're valuable here, Rohan. Not just as an IC. As a mentor, as an architect, as someone who shapes how we build. I need you thinking about that.

Rohan: Nothing right now. I'm good.
